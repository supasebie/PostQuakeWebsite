How it works

From one link to a week of posts.

Your coding agent does the thinking. PostQuake's engine does everything that has to be exact: importing, validating, rendering and scheduling.

  1. 01

    Import your product

    Paste an App Store or Google Play link, or any website: a web app, a shop, a newsletter, a portfolio. PostQuake pulls the name, pitch, icon and brand colours, and takes screenshots from the store listing or from your site itself.

  2. 02

    Learn who it's for

    A one-time, ten-question interview, then research into real reviews and forums. The result is a customer profile written in their words, with every quote sourced. Nothing is made up.

  3. 03

    Write, check, render

    Pick formats and a count. Every hook, slide and caption is written for that customer, checked against the format rules and the rest of the batch, then rendered to 1080×1920 slides and an MP4.

  4. 04

    Ship it Rolling out

    Export a zip with a posting manifest, or schedule across TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Facebook Pages through Postiz, with a dry run you approve first.

Why PostQuake

          
Built to get posted, and keep your accounts healthy.

          
Pumping out slideshows is the easy part. The hard part is doing it every week without repeating yourself, getting flagged, or making claims you can't back up.

        


        

          

            

            
Never posts the same thing twice

            
Every set is checked against its format and the rest of the batch before anything renders. Duplicate hooks and captions are rejected, and near-duplicates are flagged.

            

              
✓friends-psychic-test · 5 slides · caption 88/150

              
✕listicle-02 · hook says "5 signs", has 4 items

              
!before-after-01 · caption close to hook-story-03

            

          


          

            

            
Your own photos, no AI label

            
Drop in your own photos or licensed stock and record where each came from. Sets built only from them post without an AI label. Sets with AI imagery are labelled automatically, and provenance is never stripped.

            

              
Set A2 AI images + screenshot
AI label applied

              
Set BYour photos + screenshot
No label needed

            

          


          

            

            
Pennies per post

            
About $0.10 per AI image on your own Gemini key. Images are cached, so re-renders are free, and every paid call is logged.

          


          

            

            
Licensed music only

            
Videos only use tracks with a recorded licence. Anything else is refused before it can get you muted or struck.

          


          

            

            
Safe-zone aware

            
Text stays out of the bottom caption area and the right-hand button rail on TikTok and Reels, and it auto-fits.

          


          

            

            
No fake testimonials

            
Customer quotes have to be real and sourced. Staged chats say "dramatization" right on the slide. That keeps you on the right side of endorsement rules.

FAQ

          
Questions, answered.

          
Still curious? Find us @postquake on TikTok.

        


        

          

            Is it only for apps?
            
No. Apps were our first customer, not the limit. Paste any website: a web app, an online shop, a newsletter, a portfolio, a course or a local business. When there's no store listing, PostQuake screenshots your site on a phone for the product slides and closes each set with your domain.

          

          

            Do I need to be a developer?
            
Today PostQuake runs through a coding agent like Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. You talk to the agent in plain English and it drives PostQuake for you, but you'll need to be comfortable opening one. A web app that anyone can use is next on the roadmap.

          

          

            What does it cost to run?
            
AI images cost about $0.10 each on your own Gemini key, paid straight to Google. Everything else (slides, videos, mock screens, store cards) renders locally for free, and cached images cost nothing to re-render. Use the calculator for your numbers.

          

          

            Will my posts be labelled as AI?
            
Only the sets that contain AI-generated imagery, and those are labelled automatically wherever the platform asks. Sets built from your own photos or licensed stock, screenshots and rendered screens carry no label. PostQuake never strips or hides AI provenance.

          

          

            Which platforms does it post to?
            
TikTok (photo slideshow or video), Instagram Reels, Facebook Page video and YouTube Shorts. Scheduling goes through Postiz, and you can always export a zip with a posting manifest and post by hand.

          

          

            Won't all the posts look the same?
            
Every set gets its own angle. Across a batch, PostQuake varies the hook style, first word, screenshot, setting and caption, and the validator rejects duplicate hooks or captions before anything renders.

          

          

            Is the music safe to use?
            
PostQuake only uses tracks with a recorded licence and refuses anything else. Note that TikTok business accounts are limited to TikTok's Commercial Music Library.

          

          

            Can it write fake reviews or testimonials?
            
No. Customer quotes have to be real and sourced. Mocked chats and notifications are dramatizations, labelled on the slide, and they're never presented as real users or results.

          

          

            Who's behind PostQuake?
            
An indie team that built PostQuake to market our own game, Psychic Tournament, and kept going. Every feature gets tested on our own accounts before it reaches yours.
